# Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: May 13, 2026

qwertZnake is a typing practice game for iPad. The app is designed to work without a user account and without collecting personal data for advertising or tracking.

## Data Stored On Device

qwertZnake may store gameplay information locally on the device, such as player name entered for a local leaderboard, score, level, accuracy, WPM, selected difficulty, and custom level data.

This information is used only to provide app features such as local progress display, local leaderboards, and gameplay settings.

## Data Collection

The iPad app does not send gameplay data, names, scores, analytics, or device identifiers to our servers.

The app does not use third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies.

## Children And Students

qwertZnake is intended for learning and practice. It does not require a user account, email address, phone number, classroom identifier, or precise location.

## Network Access

The App Store version bundles the game locally inside the app. It does not need an internet connection for normal gameplay.

If you visit the qwertZnake website or support pages, standard web server logs may be created by the website host. Those logs are separate from the iPad app.
