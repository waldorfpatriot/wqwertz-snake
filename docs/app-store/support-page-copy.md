## qwertZnake Support

qwertZnake is an iPad game for practicing the 10-finger system on a German QWERTZ keyboard.

For questions, bug reports, accessibility feedback, classroom use, or feature ideas, contact:

support@qwertznake.de

Please include:
- Your iPad model
- Your iPadOS version
- Whether you used the on-screen keyboard or an external keyboard
- A short description of what happened

Privacy policy: https://qwertznake.de/privacy
