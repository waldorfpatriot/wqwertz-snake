# qwertZnake Monetization Plan

This plan is optimized for the fastest honest first release: a paid iPad app with no ads, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.

## Version 1.0 Revenue Model

- Set qwertZnake as a paid upfront app in App Store Connect.
- Recommended launch price: EUR 2.99 in Germany as the base country or region.
- Let Apple automatically generate comparable prices for other storefronts.
- Do not add ads or third-party analytics in version 1.0. This keeps the app suitable for students, keeps the privacy label simple, and avoids consent and classroom trust problems.
- Do not add subscriptions in version 1.0. The app is a focused practice tool, and the current feature set is easier to sell as a low-friction one-time purchase.

## App Store Connect Money Checklist

1. Account Holder signs the Paid Apps Agreement in App Store Connect Business.
2. Enter banking information.
3. Complete required tax forms.
4. In the app record, open Monetization > Pricing and Availability.
5. Add pricing before submitting for review.
6. Choose Germany as the base country or region.
7. Choose the EUR 2.99 price point for launch.
8. Keep availability broad unless there is a legal or support reason to exclude a storefront.
9. After approval, switch from manual release to live release only after the product page, screenshots, price, and privacy label are correct.

Apple references:

- https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-pricing/set-a-price/
- https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-agreements/sign-and-update-agreements/
- https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/provide-tax-information/tax-forms-overview/
- https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/getting-paid/overview-of-receiving-payments/

## Website Conversion Tasks

- After Apple provides the App Store URL, add a primary "Im App Store kaufen" link on `qwertznake.de`.
- Keep the browser version available as a lightweight demo, but position the iPad app as the paid offline classroom-friendly version.
- Add the App Store link to `support.html`, `privacy.html`, and the homepage menu.
- Use the first week after release for direct outreach to teachers, parents, and typing course operators in German-speaking markets.

## Later Revenue Options

- Classroom bundle: create a paid custom app offer for schools through Apple School Manager if there is institutional interest.
- Version 1.1: add more native practice modes, not monetization complexity.
- Version 1.2 or later: consider an optional "school pack" in-app purchase only if there is enough content to justify it.
